# Test map — Sign in and sign out (backend)

Every row is runnable on its own. Copy the command. The `::name` is the leading part of the test title and is the `-t` filter. Integration rows use `real-db`, served in-process by `mongodb-memory-server` (started by `tests/integration/globalSetup.ts`; nothing for the caller to launch). One scrypt hash takes about 2 seconds on a slow machine, so integration rows that sign in are slow by design (testTimeout 30000). Screen behaviour (sign-in form, Log out control, back-button behaviour, no Remember me option, browser-close wording) belongs to the frontend sub-task.
